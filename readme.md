0 Introduction: Wind and solar energy, as clean energy sources, have gained attention in the power industry due to their environmental friendliness and abundant resources. The power industry has begun to vigorously develop clean energy generation. At the same time, wind and solar energy exhibit characteristics of intermittency and randomness during power generation, which can impact the stability and reliability of the power grid after being integrated into it. Currently, microgrid systems are widely recognized as an effective means to improve the utilization rate of wind and solar energy. Incentive-based demand response (DR) refers to a strategy where users adjust their electricity consumption behavior proactively according to government or power company incentive policies, thereby alleviating the shortage of electricity supply during peak load periods. The introduction of incentive-based DR measures in microgrids can guide users to use electricity rationally and optimize the distribution of load side demand.
